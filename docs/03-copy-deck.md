Writing rules for anyone adding copy:
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Lead with the reader's problem, then the fix, then the action.
- Every section ends with a next step.
- British English. "Organisation", "programme", "licence" (noun).
- No claim without proof. If unsure, wrap it in `data-verify="what to check"`.
- No em dashes. Use a full stop or a comma.
