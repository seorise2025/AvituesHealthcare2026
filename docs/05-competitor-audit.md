# Competitor audit

**Reviewed:** 25 November 2026, live sites via Firecrawl. Homepages plus each site's staff request page or form.
**Scope:** businesses competing for UK care providers and care workers in the same services. Five came from the earlier report; two are London and framework rivals found by search.

Notes:
- Facts below are what each site **says**. None of their claims were independently checked.
- No competitor copy is reused on the Aviteus site. The decisions below take ideas about *what to explain*, not wording.
- **Not reviewed: Nurseplus** (nurseplus.co.uk). The site returned an SSL certificate error on 25 November 2026. Bypassing the certificate check was declined. Re-check before launch.

## 5. What customers still do not learn

Across all seven sites:

1. **What affects the price.** No site explains what changes an agency rate (role, time, notice, volume). Not one.
2. **What is not included.** No service page sets out exclusions.
3. **The written terms step.** Agencies must agree terms with a client before supplying staff. Only Kome hints at this.
4. **What happens after you enquire.** Only Kome sets it out step by step.
5. **How to check an agency yourself.** Nobody tells buyers they can search the NMC register themselves.
6. **Candidate fee protection.** Few sites explain that charging work-seekers is illegal, even though visa-fee scams target care workers.
7. **Proof that can be checked.** Several sites show awards, percentages or logos with no detail (OneCall24, Lopen). Kome is the exception, naming its framework number and dates.

## 6. Decisions this audit drove

| Finding | Decision on the Aviteus site |
|---|---|
| No one explains cost | Every service and sector page has a **"What affects cost and timing"** section |
| No one lists exclusions | Every service page has **Included / Not included** boxes |
| Kome's form is long (16 fields), Newcross's is too generic to triage | Employer form: **6 required fields** plus optional roles, setting and notes. Urgency and role are captured so urgent requests are flagged in the email subject |
| Pure and Kome use a privacy line, not a consent tick | Privacy line under every form. Marketing is a **separate, optional, unticked** box |
| Herd Care has no privacy wording at its form | Every form links to a privacy notice written around what the form actually collects |
| Kome publishes its framework number and dates | Framework claims stay off the site until lot numbers and expiry dates are on file (`frameworks` claim) |
| Pure's 109 Google reviews are the strongest proof in the set | Aviteus should start collecting Google reviews now. Testimonials only go live with consent (`t-*` claims) |
| Broken or vague stats (Lopen, OneCall24) damage trust | No statistics are published without evidence (claims register) |
| Pure lists 380 live jobs; Aviteus has no live feed | No job board. Role pages explain the work, and visitors register to be matched. No fake listings |
| Kome says plainly that a request is no guarantee | "Not included: a guarantee that every urgent shift can be filled", written in our own words |
| Few explain the no-fee law | No-fee notice on home, candidate, registration and thank-you pages, citing the law |
| Newcross and Pure answer buyer FAQs | FAQs on every service page, built from real buyer objections (cost, contract, cancellation, same workers) |
| Pure and Newcross lead with speed promises | No response-time promise until operations can evidence it (`h24` claim). Phone-first wording for urgent cover instead |
