How to join

Start work in five simple steps.

  1. Register online

    Fill in a short form. It takes about 10 minutes.

  2. Have a chat

    A consultant calls you to talk about your experience and the work you want.

  3. Upload documents

    ID, right to work, references and training, all in your online portal.

  4. Get checked

Questions

Questions from care workers.

How long does registration take?

The online form takes about 10 minutes. Checks usually take one to two weeks, mostly waiting for the DBS. Having your documents ready makes it faster.

Do I need my own DBS?

No. We help you apply. If you are already on the DBS Update Service, we can check it online and you may start sooner.

Can I work with Aviteus alongside another job?

Yes. Many of our workers pick up extra shifts around a permanent role. You choose when you work.

Do you sponsor visas?

We hold a Skilled Worker sponsor licence, but sponsorship depends on the role, the salary rules and our current openings. We never charge for sponsorship. Ask us about current options.
