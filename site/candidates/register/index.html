Documents to get ready

Have these to hand and you can start much sooner.

  • Passport or ID, and right to work proof
  • Two proofs of address from the last 3 months
  • National Insurance number
  • Contact details for two referees
  • Training certificates you already hold
  • DBS Update Service number, if you have one
  • Nurses: NMC pin
  • Bank details for weekly pay
