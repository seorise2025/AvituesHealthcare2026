Our vetting process

Seven checks before anyone works a shift.

CheckWhat we doHow often
1. InterviewFace-to-face or video interview with skills and scenario questions.At registration
2. Right to workPassport, share code or visa checked in line with Home Office rules.At registration and before visa expiry
3. Enhanced DBSEnhanced check with barred lists, or DBS Update Service check.Yearly, or Update Service
4. ReferencesTwo references covering the last three years, one from a clinical or care role.At registration
5. TrainingMandatory training, including safeguarding, moving and handling and basic life support.Refreshed every year
6. Occupational healthHealth questionnaire and immunisation records where the setting needs them.At registration
7. Professional registrationNMC pin and any conditions checked for every nurse.Before every booking cycle

Questions

Compliance questions.

Can I see a worker's compliance file before they arrive?

Yes. We send a worker profile with every booking. Full files are available on request for your records or an inspection.

How do you check nurses?

We check each nurse's NMC register entry, including any conditions, and confirm their clinical references and training before booking them.

What happens when a document expires?

Our system flags expiring documents early. A worker with an expired check is not booked until it is renewed.
