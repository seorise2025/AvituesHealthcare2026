Our story

Built by people who know care.

Aviteus Healthcare began as a nursing agency. Today we supply nurses, healthcare assistants and support workers to care homes, supported living services, mental health providers and the NHS.

We saw the same problems again and again. Agencies that did not answer at night. Workers sent to settings they did not understand. Compliance files that could not be found when the inspector asked.

So we built our service around three promises:

  • Answer the phone. Day or night, a real person picks up.
  • Send the right person. Matched to the setting, not just the job title.
  • Prove every check. Compliance files ready when you ask for them.
