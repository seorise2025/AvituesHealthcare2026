Why we do it the way we do

Talk to any care manager about agencies and you'll hear the same three complaints. The phone rings out when you need it most. The person who turns up has never worked anywhere like yours. And when the inspector asks how that person was checked, nobody can find the paperwork.

We built Aviteus around fixing those three things. One number for bookings, answered by people who know your service. Workers chosen for the setting, not just the job title. And if a shift is going to be hard to fill, we say so early, so you can make a plan.

We look after the people who work for us, too. Agency work only works for a care service when the workers are treated well enough to want to come back.

How we run the business

  • Net zero by 2045. Our published carbon reduction plan shows emissions falling from 493 to 348 tonnes CO2e between 2022 and 2024.
  • Modern slavery. We publish a statement setting out our approach.
  • Data protection. Registered with the Information Commissioner's Office.
  • No fees for workers. We never charge anyone for finding them work.
