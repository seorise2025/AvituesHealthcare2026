What we believe

Agency staff should make your day easier, not harder.

Care managers tell us the same things about agencies. Calls that are not answered. Workers who do not know the type of service. Paperwork that cannot be found when an inspector asks for it.

We set up Aviteus to do three things well:

  • Be easy to reach. One number for bookings, and people who know your service.
  • Send the right person. Matched to the setting, not just the job title.
  • Be honest. If a shift is going to be hard to fill, we say so early.
