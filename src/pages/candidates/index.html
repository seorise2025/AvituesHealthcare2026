Questions

Questions from care workers.

How long does it take to start?

It depends mostly on how fast your checks come back. Getting your documents ready before you apply is the best way to speed things up.

Do I have to accept every shift I am offered?

No. You choose which shifts to accept.

Can I work for Aviteus alongside another job?

Yes, as long as your other contract allows it. Many agency workers pick up extra shifts this way.

Will I get written terms?

Yes. Before you start, you will receive your terms in writing and a key information document. It explains who pays you, how much and any deductions.
