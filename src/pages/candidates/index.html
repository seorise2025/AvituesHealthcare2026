Who we're looking for

  • Healthcare assistants

    Care homes, nursing homes and hospitals.

  • Support workers

    Supported living, learning disability and mental health.

  • Registered nurses

    RGN, RMN and RNLD, mostly in nursing homes and mental health.

  • Senior carers and team leaders

    For people ready to lead a shift.

Most roles need at least six months of paid care experience in the UK.

What you get

  • You choose which shifts to accept
  • Shifts offered close to where you live
  • A consultant you can ring with questions
  • Weekly pay for the hours on your signed timesheets
  • Mandatory training and Basic Life Support at no cost
  • A uniform before your first shift
  • Help with your DBS, PVG or AccessNI application
  • A bonus when someone you refer completes their qualifying hours

Getting started

  1. Register online

    A short form. About five minutes.

  2. A call from us

    About your experience and the work you want.

  3. Apply in the portal

    The full application and your documents, uploaded online.

  4. Pre-employment checks

    Including right to work and a criminal record check. The law requires both for care work.

  5. Choose your shifts

    Once everything's done, we start offering you work.

The documents you'll need. Having them ready is the best way to speed things up.

How long until I can start?

Mostly it depends on how quickly your checks come back. Getting your documents ready before you apply makes the biggest difference.

Do I have to take every shift I'm offered?

No. You choose.

Can I work for you alongside another job?

Yes, as long as your other contract allows it.

Will I get written terms?

Yes. Before you start you'll get your terms in writing and a key information document, which explains who pays you, how much and any deductions.

{{icon:alert}}

We never charge you to find you work. UK law doesn't allow employment agencies to charge for finding people jobs, and that includes visa sponsorship. If anyone asks you for money in our name, don't pay, and email info@aviteushealthcare.co.uk.
