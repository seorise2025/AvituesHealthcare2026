Documents to get ready

You do not need these to register. Having them ready makes your checks faster.

  • Proof of your right to work: a UK or Irish passport, or a share code if you have an eVisa
  • Photo ID
  • Proof of address from the last 3 months
  • National Insurance number
  • Contact details for two referees
  • Training certificates you already hold
  • Your DBS Update Service details, if you have them
  • Nurses: your NMC PIN

After you register

What happens next.

  1. We contact you

    A consultant calls to talk about your experience and the work you want.

  2. Application

    You complete a full application in our online portal.

  3. Interview

    A conversation about your experience and how you would handle real situations.

  4. Pre-employment checks

    Including right to work and a criminal record check. The law requires both for care work.

  5. First shifts

    Once everything is complete, we start offering you work.

Questions

Registration questions.

Does it cost anything to register?

No. UK employment agencies are not allowed to charge work-seekers a fee for finding them work. If anyone asks you for money in our name, do not pay and email info@aviteushealthcare.co.uk.

How long do the checks take?

Most of the time is the criminal record check. If you are already on the DBS Update Service and the check is at the right level for the work, things can move faster.

Can I register if I have no care experience?

Most agency shifts need some paid care experience. Register anyway and tell us. We will be honest about whether we can offer you work.

Do you sponsor visas?

We hold a Skilled Worker sponsor licence, but sponsorship depends on the role meeting the current Home Office rules. Many care roles do not. We never charge for sponsorship. Never pay anyone who offers you a sponsored job.
