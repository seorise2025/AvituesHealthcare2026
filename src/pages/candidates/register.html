Documents to get ready

You don't need these to register, but having them ready speeds up your checks.

  • Right to work: a UK or Irish passport, or a share code if you have an eVisa
  • Photo ID
  • Proof of address from the last three months
  • National Insurance number
  • Details of two referees
  • Training certificates you already hold
  • DBS Update Service details, if you have them
  • Nurses: your NMC PIN

Questions about registering

Does it cost anything?

No. UK employment agencies aren't allowed to charge you for finding you work. If anyone asks you for money in our name, don't pay and email info@aviteushealthcare.co.uk.

How long do the checks take?

The criminal record check usually takes the longest. If you're on the DBS Update Service and your certificate is at the right level, things can move faster.

Can I register without care experience?

Most agency shifts need some paid care experience. Register anyway and tell us. We'll be straight with you about whether we can offer you work.

Do you sponsor visas?

We hold a Skilled Worker sponsor licence, but sponsorship depends on the role meeting the current Home Office rules, and many care roles don't. We never charge for sponsorship, and you should never pay anyone who offers you a sponsored job.
