Timesheets

Ask the nurse or manager in charge to sign your timesheet at the end of every shift. We can't pay hours that haven't been signed for.

{{icon:file}} Download the timesheet (PDF)Worker portal

Before you send it

  • Every shift has a start time, finish time and break
  • The person in charge has signed and printed their name
  • The photo or scan is clear, with nothing cut off

Your consultant will tell you the weekly deadline. For questions about pay, use the contact form and choose "Timesheet or pay".
