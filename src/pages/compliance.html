Our vetting process

Checks completed before anyone works.

CheckWhat it involves
InterviewExperience, values and how the person would handle real situations.
Right to workChecked using the Home Office online service, a certified digital identity provider, or original documents, as the Home Office employer guide allows.
Criminal record checkEnhanced DBS with the adults' barred list for work in England and Wales. PVG scheme membership for regulated roles in Scotland.
ReferencesTwo references, including one from a care or clinical role.
TrainingMandatory training checked for dates, with refreshers tracked.
Fitness to workA health declaration and immunisation records where the setting requires them.
Professional registrationNMC register checked for every nurse, including any conditions. Social Care Wales registration for social care workers in Wales.

Questions

Compliance questions.

What should I check before booking any agency worker?

Right to work, a criminal record check at the right level, references, training dates and, for nurses, the NMC register. Our checklist for care managers explains each one.

Can I see a worker's file before they arrive?

Yes. Ask us when you book. We share what you need for your records in line with the worker's privacy notice.

Do you charge workers for checks or training?

Workers are never charged for finding them work. Ask us how DBS fees and training are handled.

Questions about our checks?

Call us before you book. We will walk you through them.
