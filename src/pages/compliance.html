How we check the people we send

You stay responsible for everyone who works in your service, so you should be able to see what checks were done. This page sets out our vetting, our company registrations and the policies we publish.

Checks done before anyone works a shift

CheckWhat it involves
InterviewExperience, values, and how the person would handle real situations.
Right to workUsing the Home Office online service, a certified digital identity provider, or original documents, as the Home Office employer guide allows.
Criminal record checkEngland and Wales: Enhanced DBS with the adults' barred list. Scotland: PVG scheme membership. Northern Ireland: Enhanced AccessNI check.
ReferencesTwo, including one from a care or clinical role.
TrainingMandatory training checked for dates, with refreshers tracked.
Fitness to workA health declaration, plus immunisation records where the setting needs them.
RegistrationNMC register checked for every nurse, including conditions. Social Care Wales and NISCC registration for social care workers in Wales and Northern Ireland.

You can ask for a worker's compliance details before or after a shift. Contact us and choose "Compliance file".

Compliance questions

What should I check before booking any agency worker?

Right to work, a criminal record check at the right level, references, training dates and, for nurses, the NMC register. Our checklist for care managers goes through each one.

Can I see a worker's file before they arrive?

Yes. Ask when you book. We share what you need for your records, in line with the worker's privacy notice.

Do workers pay for their own checks or training?

Workers are never charged for finding them work. Ask us how DBS fees and training are handled.
