When you call, have this ready

  • Role needed (nurse, HCA, support worker)
  • Shift start and finish time
  • Service address and postcode
  • Any key skills, e.g. PEG, hoist, PBS
  • Who the worker should report to
What happens after you call

From phone call to staff at the door.

  1. You call

    A consultant takes the details. No call centre. No script.

  2. We call our workers

    We start with people who live near you and know your type of service.

  3. We confirm the name

    You get the worker's name and arrival time by text or email.

  4. They arrive ready

    In uniform, with ID, checked and trained. We follow up after the shift.

Questions

Emergency cover questions.

Is your line really staffed at night?

Yes. Out of hours, your call goes to a member of our team who can book staff. It does not go to a message service.

Do urgent shifts cost more?

Rates for short-notice, night and bank holiday shifts can be higher. We always tell you the rate before you confirm the booking.

What if you cannot fill the shift?

We tell you early and honestly, so you can make other plans. We do not leave you waiting for a call that never comes.
