The service

Who it is for and what it covers.

Included

  • Cover starting today or tomorrow
  • Day shifts, nights, long days, sleep-ins and waking nights
  • Nurses, healthcare assistants, senior carers and support workers
  • Care homes, nursing homes, supported living and mental health services

Not included

  • A guarantee that every urgent shift can be filled
  • Workers who do not meet the checks for your setting, however urgent
  • Doctors, therapists and other professions we do not supply
How it works

From your call to staff at the door.

  1. You call

    We take the details and agree the rate for the shift.

  2. We contact workers

    We start with people near you who have worked in your type of service.

  3. We call you back

    With a name and an arrival time, or an honest update if the shift is proving hard to fill.

  4. The worker arrives

    They report to the person you named. They arrive in uniform.

Can you always fill an urgent shift?

No agency can promise that. We will tell you early and honestly how the search is going, so you can make other plans if you need to.

Do urgent shifts cost more?

Short-notice, night and bank holiday shifts are usually charged at a higher rate. We confirm the rate before the booking is made.

Do I need an account first?

We need written terms with you before we supply anyone. If you are new, we can agree them quickly by email while we start the search.
