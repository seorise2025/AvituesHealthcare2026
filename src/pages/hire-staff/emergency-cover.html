Have this to hand when you ring

  • The role: nurse, HCA, senior carer or support worker
  • Start and finish time
  • Address and postcode
  • Any must-have skills
  • Who they report to when they arrive

Who uses urgent cover

It is for care home managers, nurses in charge and supported living coordinators whose rota has just fallen apart. The staffing line is answered by a person at any hour, weekends and bank holidays included.

What we can cover

  • Shifts starting today or tomorrow
  • Days, nights, long days, sleep-ins and waking nights
  • Nurses, healthcare assistants, senior carers and support workers

What we can't promise

  • That every urgent shift can be filled. Nobody can honestly promise that.
  • To send anyone whose checks aren't complete, however urgent it is.
  • Doctors, therapists or other professions we don't supply.

Cost and timing

Short-notice, night and bank holiday shifts are usually charged at a higher rate. We confirm the rate on the phone before we book anyone.

Timing depends mostly on how much notice you give, where you are and which role you need. Nurses, especially RMNs, are the hardest to find at short notice.

If you're new to us, we need written terms before we supply anyone. We can agree them by email while the search is under way.

What happens after you call

  1. We take the details

    And agree the rate for the shift.

  2. We ring round

    Starting with people nearby who have worked in your kind of service.

  3. We call you back

    With a name and arrival time, or an honest update.

  4. They arrive

    And report to whoever you named, in uniform.

If the same gaps keep coming back

Most urgent calls come from the same few shifts each month. If that's you, block book those shifts in advance with the same workers. It's calmer for everyone, and usually cheaper.
