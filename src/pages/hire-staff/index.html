Cost

What affects the price of agency staff.

We agree rates with you in writing before anyone works a shift. These are the things that change them.

The role

A registered nurse costs more than a healthcare assistant or support worker.

When the shift is

Nights, weekends and bank holidays cost more than weekday days.

Notice

Same-day requests can cost more and are harder to fill than shifts booked ahead.

How much you book

Block bookings and long lines are easier to plan than one-off shifts.

Skills needed

Specialist skills narrow the pool of people who can work the shift.

How you buy

NHS buyers on HealthTrust Europe or NHS Wales frameworks book at framework rates.

What you can expect

How every booking works.

  • Terms in writing first. A short client agreement with your rates, before we supply anyone.
  • Matched to your setting. We ask about the people you support, not just the job title.
  • Written confirmation. Worker name, role and start time for every booking.
  • A worker profile. Sent to you before the shift.
  • A named account manager. One person who knows your service.
  • If it does not work out. Tell us. We will not send that worker to you again and we will look for a replacement.

Questions

Before you book.

Do I have to sign a contract?

Yes, a short one. The law requires an agency to agree terms with a client in writing before it supplies staff. It sets out the rates, how bookings and cancellations work, and any fee if you later employ a worker directly.

Is there a minimum booking?

No. You can book a single shift once your client agreement is in place.

Can we offer one of your workers a permanent job?

Yes. The terms for this are set out in your client agreement. Ask us before you make an offer.

Which areas do you cover?

Our office is in Harrow, North West London. Send us your postcode and we will tell you honestly whether we can cover it.
