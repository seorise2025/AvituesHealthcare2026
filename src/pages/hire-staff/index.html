Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to sign a contract to book a shift?

No. We set up a simple client account with agreed rates. You can book one shift and stop there. Framework clients book under their framework terms.

How do you set your rates?

Rates depend on the role, the time of the shift and the notice given. Nights, weekends and bank holidays cost more. We give you the rate before you confirm.

Can workers move to a permanent role with us?

Yes. Many of our clients hire workers they first met through us. Ask us about our temporary to permanent terms.

Which areas do you cover?

Our office is in Harrow, North West London. We supply services across England, Scotland and Wales. Share your postcode and we will confirm cover.
