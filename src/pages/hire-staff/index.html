What affects the price

We don't publish a price list, because the same shift can cost quite different amounts depending on a handful of things. What we do instead is agree the rates with you in writing before anyone starts. These are the things that move the number:

  • The role. A registered nurse costs more than a healthcare assistant or support worker.
  • When the shift is. Nights, weekends and bank holidays are charged at higher rates.
  • How much notice you give. Same-day requests can cost more, and they are harder to fill.
  • How much you book. A run of shifts is easier to plan than scattered single ones.
  • Specialist skills. The more specific the requirement, the fewer people can do it.
  • How you buy. NHS buyers booking through HealthTrust Europe or NHS Wales frameworks pay framework rates.

Before you book

Do I have to sign a contract?

Yes, a short one. The law requires an agency to agree terms with a client in writing before it supplies staff. It covers rates, how bookings and cancellations work, and any fee if you later take on one of our workers directly.

Is there a minimum booking?

No. Once the agreement is in place you can book a single shift.

Can we offer one of your workers a permanent job?

Yes. The terms are in your client agreement. Talk to us before you make the offer.

Where do you cover?

Anywhere in the UK. Send your postcode and we'll tell you honestly how much choice we have nearby.
