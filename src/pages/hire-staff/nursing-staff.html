Agency nurses

Registered nurses for the shifts that need one.

A nursing home cannot run a shift without a nurse on site. We supply RGN, RMN and RNLD nurses for days, nights and weekends.

Every nurse's registration is checked on the NMC register before we book them.

Which nurse do you need?

RGNNursing homes, older people, physical health
RMNMental health services, dementia units
RNLDLearning disability and complex care

Included

  • RGN, RMN and RNLD nurses
  • Day, night and long day shifts, single or block booked
  • NMC register check, including any conditions on practice
  • Requests for specific clinical skills, checked against the nurse's training

Not included

  • Midwives, health visitors and doctors
  • Clinical decisions outside the nurse's competence or your policies
  • Any nurse whose NMC registration is lapsed or restricted for the work

Clinical skills you can ask for

  • Medication rounds
  • PEG feeding
  • Catheter care
  • Wound care
  • Diabetes care
  • End of life care
How it works

Booking a nurse.

  1. Tell us the shift

    Nurse type, hours, number of residents and any clinical tasks.

  2. We check the match

    Registration, experience in your setting and the skills you asked for.

  3. You know who is coming

    Name and arrival time before the shift.

  4. Handover and shift

    Your team hands over as usual. The nurse signs your timesheet at the end.

Clinical skills

Specific skills narrow the number of nurses who can take the shift.

Questions

Agency nurse questions.

How do you check a nurse's registration?

We check the public NMC register for every nurse, including the type of registration and any conditions, before we book them.

Can an agency nurse be the nurse in charge?

Yes, if you book them for that role and they have the experience. Tell us when you book so we match the right nurse and brief them.

Do you supply nurses in Scotland?

Yes. Nurse agencies in Scotland must be registered with the Care Inspectorate, and we are.
