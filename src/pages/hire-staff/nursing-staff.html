Agency nurses for care and mental health services

A nursing home can't run a shift without a registered nurse on site. We supply RGN, RMN and RNLD nurses for days, nights and weekends, and we think carefully about which nurse goes where.

Which nurse do you need?

RGN (adult)Nursing homes, older people, physical health, end of life care
RMN (mental health)Mental health wards and rehab, and dementia units where behaviour is a big part of the care
RNLD (learning disability)Learning disability nursing, complex care, some supported living services

Before we book any nurse, we check their entry on the NMC register, including the type of registration and any conditions on their practice.

Clinical skills you can ask for

Medication rounds, PEG feeding, catheter care, wound care, diabetes care and end of life care. Tell us when you book and we'll only offer nurses with recent experience of it.

Not something we do

  • Nurses whose registration is lapsed, or restricted for the work you need
  • Clinical work outside the nurse's competence or your own policies

Cost and timing

RMNs and RNLDs are harder to find than RGNs in most areas, so give us as much notice as you can. Night, weekend and bank holiday rates are higher. Rates are agreed in writing before the first booking.

Booking a nurse

  1. Tell us about the shift

    Which nurse, the hours, how many residents, any clinical tasks.

  2. We look for the match

    Registration, experience in your type of service, and the skills you asked for.

  3. You know who's coming

    Their name and start time, before the shift.

  4. Handover as usual

    The nurse signs your timesheet at the end of the shift.

Questions about agency nurses

Can an agency nurse be nurse in charge?

Yes, if you book them for that and they have the experience. Tell us when you book, so we can choose the right nurse and brief them.

Can I check a nurse's registration myself?

Yes. The NMC register is public and free to search by name or PIN. It is worth doing for any agency nurse, ours included.

Do you supply nurses in Scotland and Northern Ireland?

Yes. We are registered with the Care Inspectorate in Scotland and RQIA in Northern Ireland, as nurse agencies there must be.
