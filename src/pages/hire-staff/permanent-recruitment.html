Permanent recruitment for care services

Every good permanent hire is one less shift you need agency for. We take a proper brief, search, interview and check references, and send you a short list of people we would be happy to employ ourselves.

Start a search020 3488 8172

Roles we recruit

Home managers, deputy managers, clinical leads, registered nurses, senior support workers, team leaders and care assistants.

What we do

  • Agree a written brief with you: the service, the team, the hours, the pay
  • Search the people we already know, then advertise if we need to
  • Interview and take references before you see a CV
  • Send a short list, with a note on each person
  • Help with the offer, and keep in touch through the notice period

What we don't do

  • Make the hiring decision. That's always yours.
  • Your own employer checks on day one, such as the right to work check.

Fees and timing

Permanent fees are usually based on the role's starting salary. We agree the fee in writing before we start.

Registered managers and RMNs take longest to find. Most good candidates also have to work a notice period, often four weeks or more, so start early.

Questions

Do you charge if we don't hire anyone?

Your terms will say exactly when a fee is due. We'll go through them with you before we start.

Can we take on an agency worker we already know?

Yes. It's covered in your client agreement. Talk to us before you make the offer.
