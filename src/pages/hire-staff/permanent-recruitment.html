Cost and timing

What affects the fee and the time to hire.

The salary

Permanent fees are usually based on the role's starting salary. We agree the fee in writing before we start.

How rare the skills are

Registered managers and RMNs take longer to find than care assistants.

Your notice periods

Most good candidates must work notice, often four weeks or more.

Questions

Permanent recruitment questions.

Do you charge if we do not hire anyone?

Your terms will say exactly when a fee is due. Ask us to explain them before we start.

Can we hire an agency worker we already know?

Yes. Hiring a temporary worker into a permanent role is covered in your client agreement. Talk to us before you make an offer.

How long does a search take?

It depends on the role and your location. We give you an honest estimate when we agree the brief.
