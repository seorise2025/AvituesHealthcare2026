Temporary staffing

Plan your cover. Keep the same faces.

Book temporary staff for holidays, training days, vacancies and new care packages. Block book the same workers so the people you support see familiar faces.

Three ways to book

  • Single shifts. One-off cover for leave or training.
  • Block bookings. The same worker on set shifts for weeks.
  • Long lines. Ongoing cover for a vacancy while you recruit.
Who it is for

For care managers who can see the gap coming.

Temporary staffing suits any service with a known gap: annual leave, maternity cover, a vacancy, a new resident with higher needs, or a new contract starting.

Included

  • Registered nurses (RGN, RMN, RNLD)
  • Healthcare assistants, senior carers, support workers and team leaders
  • Days, nights, long days, sleep-ins and waking nights
  • Weekend and bank holiday shifts
  • Requests for workers you have used before, where they are free

Not included

  • Running your rota or supervising workers on shift. That stays with your team
  • Roles outside nursing and care support
  • Workers starting before their checks are complete
How it works

Four steps from request to rota.

  1. Share your gaps

    Send the dates, shift times and roles. A spreadsheet or rota photo is fine.

  2. Agree terms and rates

    A short written agreement, once. After that you just book.

  3. We match workers

    For block bookings we look for people who can do every shift in the run.

  4. Confirmed before the shift

    We tell you who is coming. You get a worker profile in advance.

Cost and timing

What changes the cost.

Role

Nurse rates are higher than care and support rates.

Shift pattern

Nights, weekends and bank holidays carry higher rates. Sleep-ins are often charged differently from waking hours.

Notice and volume

Booking a run of shifts early gives us the best chance of keeping the same workers.

We agree rates in writing before any shift. There are no extra charges that are not in your client agreement.

Questions

Temporary staffing questions.

What is the minimum booking?

One shift. There is no minimum once your client agreement is in place.

Can we try a worker before a block booking?

Yes. Book a single shift first. If they fit your service, ask us to book them for the run.

What happens if we need to cancel a shift?

Cancellation terms are set out in your client agreement. Tell us as early as you can so the worker can find other work.

How do timesheets work?

The worker brings a timesheet for the person in charge to sign at the end of the shift. We invoice from the signed timesheets.
