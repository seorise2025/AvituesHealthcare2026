Temporary staff, booked before the gap becomes a crisis

If you can see a gap coming, such as annual leave, maternity cover, a vacancy or a new resident with higher needs, book it now. Booking ahead gives us the best chance of sending the same people every time.

  • Registered nurses (RGN, RMN, RNLD)
  • Healthcare assistants, senior carers, support workers and team leaders
  • Days, nights, long days, sleep-ins and waking nights, including weekends and bank holidays
  • Requests for workers you've had before, if they're free

What stays with you

  • Running the rota and supervising staff on shift
  • Induction to your building and your residents on the day

Cost

Nurse rates are higher than care and support rates. Nights, weekends and bank holidays cost more, and sleep-ins are often charged differently from waking hours.

Everything is in your client agreement before the first shift. We invoice from the timesheets your team signs, so you can check every hour against your rota.

From request to rota

  1. Send your gaps

    Dates, times and roles. A photo of the rota is fine.

  2. Agree terms once

    A short written agreement. After that, you just book.

  3. We match workers

    For block bookings, people who can do the whole run.

  4. You know who's coming

    Before each shift, with a short profile.

Common questions

Can we try someone before a block booking?

Yes. Book a single shift first. If they fit, ask us to book them for the run.

What if we need to cancel?

Cancellation terms are in your client agreement. Let us know as early as you can, so the worker can find other work.

How do timesheets work?

The worker brings a timesheet for the person in charge to sign at the end of the shift. We invoice from the signed sheets.
