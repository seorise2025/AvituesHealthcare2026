How a booking works

  1. You call or send the form

    Tell us the role, the dates and the type of service.

  2. We agree the terms

    If you're new to us, we agree rates in writing first. The law requires it, and it means no surprises on the invoice.

  3. We find the person

    We contact workers who live close enough and have done this kind of work before.

  4. You hear back

    With a name and a start time (confirmed in writing), or with an honest update if the shift is proving difficult.

When you call, we'll ask you

  • Which role you need, and how many
  • What kind of service it is, and who the person will support
  • The dates and shift times
  • Any skills they must have, such as hoist use, PEG feeding or positive behaviour support
  • The postcode, and who they should report to on arrival

Questions we get asked

How quickly can you get someone to us?

It depends on the role, the time and where you are. A healthcare assistant in a well-connected town is far easier to find at short notice than an RMN in a rural area. Ring as early as you can, and we'll tell you honestly how it's looking.

What does agency staff cost?

The hourly rate depends on the role, the time of day, the day of the week and how much notice you give. Nights, weekends and bank holidays cost more. We agree the rates with you in writing before anyone works a shift.

Do we need to sign anything first?

Yes, a short client agreement. An agency has to agree terms with you in writing before it supplies staff. Once it's in place, you can book one shift or a hundred.

Where do you cover?

We supply care services across the UK from our office in Harrow. Give us your postcode and we'll tell you who we have nearby.

Can we have the same workers each time?

Yes, whenever they're free. Block booking is the most reliable way to keep the same people.
