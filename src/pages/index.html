Why care managers call us

An empty shift is a risk to the people you care for.

Sickness, holidays and vacancies do not wait for office hours. When cover falls through, residents still need their medication, their meals and their personal care on time.

We work around three problems care managers tell us about most.

How we work with care providers

"I can't get hold of anyone."

How we work: one phone number for bookings. It is answered by a person 24 hours a day, including weekends and bank holidays.

"The worker didn't know our type of service."

How we work: we ask about the setting and the people you support, not just the job title, and we match on experience.

"I need to show my inspector the checks."

How we work: right to work, criminal record checks, references and training are completed before anyone is offered work. You can ask for a worker's compliance file at any time.

How booking works

From your call to a filled shift.

  1. You tell us what you need

    Call us or send the online form. It takes about two minutes.

  2. We agree the details

    Role, hours, skills and rates are agreed with you before we book anyone.

  3. We find the right person

    We contact workers whose experience fits your setting and who can reach you.

  4. You know who is coming

    We tell you who is coming and when. You get it in writing for every booking.

Questions

Questions care providers ask us.

How quickly can you send someone?

It depends on the role, the time and where you are. Call us as early as you can. We will tell you straight away if a shift looks hard to fill, so you can plan.

What does agency staff cost?

The hourly rate depends on the role, the time of day, the day of the week and how much notice you give. Nights, weekends and bank holidays usually cost more. We agree rates with you before any booking.

Do I need a contract to book one shift?

Before we supply anyone, the law requires us to agree terms with you in writing. That is a short client agreement. Once it is in place you can book one shift or many.

Where are you based?

Our office is in Harrow, North West London. Tell us the postcode of your service and we will tell you honestly whether we can cover it.

Can we have the same workers each time?

Where the worker is free, yes. Regular faces help the people you support and cut handover time. Ask about block bookings.
