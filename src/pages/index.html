Questions

Common questions from care providers.

How quickly can you send staff?

For urgent requests we start calling workers as soon as you hang up. Many same-day shifts are confirmed within a few hours. Book as early as you can and we will tell you straight away if a shift is hard to fill.

What checks do your staff go through?

Every worker has right to work checks, an enhanced DBS, two references, an interview, mandatory training and occupational health clearance before their first shift. Nurses also have their NMC pin checked. You can ask for any worker's compliance file.

Are you on any NHS frameworks?

Yes. We are an approved supplier on HealthTrust Europe and NHS Wales frameworks. Speak to us about the lots we cover and how to book through your framework.

Do you only cover London?

No. Our head office is in Harrow, North West London. We supply care providers across England, Scotland and Wales. Tell us your postcode and we will confirm cover for your area.

Can we book the same worker every time?

Yes, where they are available. Regular faces mean better care and less handover. Ask for a block booking or tell us your preferred workers.

What if a worker is not right for our service?

Tell us. We will look at what went wrong, and we will not send that worker to you again. We will find a replacement as fast as we can.
