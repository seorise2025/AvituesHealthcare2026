What you'll need

  • Current NMC registration, with no conditions that stop you doing the work
  • Post-registration experience in services like the ones you want to work in
  • The right to work in the UK
  • Two references, one of them from a clinical manager

The shifts

Shifts are usually in nursing homes, mental health or learning disability services. You might be the only nurse on shift leading a team of carers, or working alongside the service's own nurses. We'll tell you which before you accept.

How pay works

Each shift has its own hourly rate, based on the setting, the time, the day and the location. You'll see it before you accept.

Questions from nurses

Can I do agency shifts alongside my NHS job?

Often, yes, but check your NHS contract first. Some trusts have rules about their own staff doing agency work.

Do you help with revalidation?

Revalidation is your responsibility as a nurse. Ask your consultant what records of the shifts you've worked with us could help.

How do you check my registration?

We check the public NMC register before booking you, including the type of registration and any conditions.
