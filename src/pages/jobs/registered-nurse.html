What you need

  • Current NMC registration with no conditions that stop you doing the work
  • Post-registration experience in a setting like the ones you want to work in
  • The right to work in the UK
  • Two references, including one from a clinical manager

What the shifts involve

Shifts are usually in nursing homes, mental health or learning disability services. You might be the only nurse on shift, leading a team of carers, or working alongside the service's own nurses. We tell you which before you accept.

How pay works

Each shift has its own hourly rate, based on the setting, the time, the day and the location. You see the rate before you accept.

Questions

Agency nursing questions.

Can I do agency shifts alongside my NHS job?

Often yes, but check your NHS contract first. Some trusts have rules about agency work for their own staff.

Do you help with revalidation?

Revalidation is your responsibility as a nurse. Ask your consultant what records of the shifts you worked through us can help you.

How do you check my registration?

We check the public NMC register before booking you, including the type of registration and any conditions.
