Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your information
  • correct information that is wrong
  • delete your information
  • limit how we use it
  • stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests
  • give it to you in a format you can pass to someone else

You can withdraw consent for marketing at any time. To make a request, email us. We will reply within one month. There is no charge in most cases.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. Email info@aviteushealthcare.co.uk with "Data protection complaint" in the subject. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you what we are doing about it.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
