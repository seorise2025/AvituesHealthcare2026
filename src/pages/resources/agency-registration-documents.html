For care workers

Documents you need to join a care agency.

By Aviteus Healthcare. Checked against official guidance on 25 November 2026.

The checks before your first shift take time. Most delays come from missing documents. Get these ready and you can start sooner.

1. Proof of your right to work

Every agency must check that you are allowed to do the work before you start. What you show depends on your status:

  • British or Irish citizens: your passport. A British passport can be current or expired. Some agencies use a certified digital identity service, which checks your passport online.
  • Everyone else: you will usually need a share code from the Home Office "prove your right to work" service, plus your date of birth. The agency uses it to view your eVisa online.

Get your share code online at GOV.UK. Share codes expire, so get a new one when the agency asks for it.

2. Photo ID and proof of address

Usually a passport or photo driving licence, plus one or two documents showing your current address, such as a bank statement or council tax bill from the last three months.

3. Your National Insurance number

Needed for pay and tax.

4. Two referees

Names, job titles and work email addresses for two people who managed you. For care roles, at least one should be from a care or clinical job.

5. Criminal record check details

  • England and Wales: most care roles need an Enhanced DBS check with the adults' barred list. If you are on the DBS Update Service, have your certificate number ready.
  • Scotland: people doing regulated roles must be members of the PVG scheme. This became a legal requirement on 1 April 2025.

6. Training certificates

Bring what you have, with dates: for example safeguarding, moving and handling, basic life support and infection control. Expired certificates will need refreshing.

7. Professional registration

  • Nurses: your NMC PIN. The agency will check the public NMC register.
  • Working in Wales: most social care workers must register with Social Care Wales within six months of starting work in care.

8. Bank details

So you can be paid. Check the account is in your own name.

{{icon:alert}}

You should never pay to join an agency. UK employment agencies are not allowed to charge you a fee for finding you work. Be very careful with anyone who asks for money for a job or a visa.

Questions

Can I use my old biometric residence permit?

Employers now use the Home Office online service to check most people who are not British or Irish citizens. You will usually need a share code to prove your right to work.

Do I need a new DBS check for every agency?

Not always. If you are on the DBS Update Service and your existing certificate is at the right level for the work, the agency may be able to use it.
