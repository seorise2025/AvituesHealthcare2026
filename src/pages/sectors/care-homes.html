What care home managers tell us

Three gaps we help close.

No nurse for the night

A nursing home cannot open a shift without a nurse. Tell us as soon as you know and we focus on RGNs who already work nights in care homes.

Strangers on the floor

People living with dementia often cope better with familiar faces. Block book the same workers for your regular gaps.

Questions from inspectors

Your inspector will expect you to know how agency staff were checked. Ask us for the details before the shift.

Questions

Care home staffing questions.

Can you supply a nurse for tonight?

Call us straight away. We will tell you quickly whether we have a nurse who can reach you, and keep you updated while we look.

What should I check before booking agency staff?

Right to work, a criminal record check at the right level, references, training records and, for nurses, the NMC register. Our checklist for care managers covers each one.

Can we ask for the same carers each week?

Yes, where they are free. Block bookings are the best way to keep the same workers.
