We understand care home pressures

Three problems we solve for home managers.

Nurse gaps on nights

A nursing home cannot open a shift without a nurse on site. We keep a pool of RGNs who work nights and know care home routines.

Strangers on the floor

Residents with dementia do better with familiar faces. We send the same workers back to your home wherever we can.

Inspection pressure

CQC will ask how you check agency staff. We give you a profile for every worker before they arrive, and full files on request.

Questions

Care home staffing questions.

Can you supply nurses for a nursing home at short notice?

Yes. Call our 24/7 line and we will start with RGNs who live near you and have care home experience. We will tell you quickly if a shift will be hard to fill.

What should I check before booking agency staff?

Check the worker's DBS, right to work, training records and, for nurses, their NMC pin. We send a profile before every shift. See our care home agency checklist.

Can we request the same carers each week?

Yes. Block bookings let you keep the same workers on regular shifts, which helps residents and saves handover time.
