What home managers usually call us about

No nurse for the night shift

A nursing home can't open a shift without a nurse. Ring as soon as you know. RGNs who already work nights in care homes are the people we try first.

Too many unfamiliar faces

People living with dementia often find new faces unsettling. If the same shifts keep falling short, block book them, and we'll keep sending the same workers wherever we can.

An inspection coming up

Your inspector will expect you to know how agency staff were checked. Ask us for the details before the shift, not after.

Training our care home staff hold

Safeguarding adults, moving and handling, basic life support, infection prevention and control, medication awareness, dementia awareness and food hygiene.

What's covered

  • Residential, nursing and dementia care homes
  • Days, nights, long days and weekends
  • Single shifts, block bookings and long lines
  • Specific skills on request, such as hoist use or PEG feeding

Not covered

  • Domestic, kitchen or maintenance staff
  • Supervision on shift, which stays with your nurse or senior in charge

Cost and timing

Nurse shifts cost more than carer shifts and are harder to fill at short notice. Nights and weekends cost more than weekday days. Homes that are difficult to reach by public transport have fewer people available, so a little extra notice helps.

We agree rates in writing before the first shift, and invoice from the timesheets your team signs.

Care home questions

Can you find a nurse for tonight?

Ring us straight away. We'll tell you quickly whether there's a nurse who can reach you, and keep you updated while we look.

What should I check before booking agency staff?

Right to work, a criminal record check at the right level, references, training dates and, for nurses, the NMC register. Our checklist for care managers goes through each one.

Can we ask for the same carers each week?

Yes, when they're free. Block booking is the most reliable way to keep them.

Short-staffed at your home?

Tell us the shift and we'll tell you what we can do.
