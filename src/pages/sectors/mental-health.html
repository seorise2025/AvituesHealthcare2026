Staff for mental health services

Mental health work needs people with the right training and the right temperament: calm, observant and firm when they need to be. We supply RMNs, mental health healthcare assistants and support workers.

Request staff020 3488 8172

Settings

Mental health wards, locked and step-down rehabilitation, community and crisis services, and older adult units.

Before we book anyone, we'll ask

  • About the patient group, and the observation levels on the ward
  • Which physical intervention method your service uses, so we only offer staff trained in it
  • Whether you need a male or female worker for any observations
  • Who the worker reports to, and how handover works

Our mental health staff are trained in de-escalation, enhanced observations, safeguarding adults and Mental Health Act awareness.

Not something we provide

  • Psychiatrists, psychologists or approved mental health professionals
  • Secure transport or escorts
  • Physical intervention by anyone not trained in your approved method

Cost and timing

RMNs are among the hardest nurses to find, so book ahead wherever you can. One-to-one and two-to-one observations take more staff hours, and a specific training requirement narrows who we can offer.

Rates are agreed in writing before the first shift.
